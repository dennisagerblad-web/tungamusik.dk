Dennis Agerblad Band



Dennis Agerblad Band is a trash drag band with 4-5 members.

Dennis Agerblad Band is mainly Dennis Agerblad who is helped by his friends Tove Hansen, Gina La Mirage, Søde Car O'lina & Delores Düschess La Cour (all known from the performance groupe www.dunst.dk) We sing and perform to electronic music. From gentle tranny-chorus to naughthy songs from real life. Among other things, they offer, the quiet classical pedophile song "Knud Fest & Sjov" (Knud Party & Fun) and in the more dansable genre, there is the festive "Bol Mig I Mit Numsehul"(Fuck Me In My Butthole).
