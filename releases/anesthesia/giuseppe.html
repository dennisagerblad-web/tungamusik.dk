Giuseppe De Bellis


Giuseppe De Bellis, born in Italy , has been writing music since 1985 and has a long list of releases on labels such as Blue Room Released, 10 Kilo, T.I.P. rec , Cosmic Communication, Society, Bubble Sound, Hypnotic/Cleopatra , Iboga Records, Kontangens Rec., Phisteria & Tunga Music. Since 1987 living in København, Denmark.
